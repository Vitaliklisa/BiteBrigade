Support

Questions about rooms, invitations, voting, or your information? Contact the Bite Brigade support address.

Contact

Email bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com. Include the email address on your account and a description of the issue. Do not send your password.

Common questions

I cannot see a room

Sign in with the account invited to the room, then check your invitations. Crew-shared rooms are available to current members of that crew.

My vote or room details did not update

Check your network connection and reopen the room. If the problem continues, contact support with the room name and the account email you used.

I want to ask about or delete my information

Email bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com from your account address. Requests may require identity verification. See the privacy policy for more information.
