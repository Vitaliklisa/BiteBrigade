Privacy Policy

This draft explains what information Table Talk uses to help groups decide where to eat.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy describes Table Talk, a restaurant decision app. Before public release, the service operator should verify it against the production configuration and applicable law. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to datedawnsupport@gmail.com.

1. Information handled

The configured Firebase project may store your account identifier, email address and display name; rooms, meal times, descriptions, restaurant options and details; votes and notes; participant roles, crew memberships and invitations; and in-app notification records about invitations and room activity.

Do not submit information you do not want shared with people in the room or crew.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, create and share restaurant decisions, collect votes, show in-app updates, and protect the service.

3. Sharing

Room information is available to its creator and accepted participants. Information in a room shared with a crew is also available to that crew's members. Invitation email addresses are used to address invitations. Room content is not public by default.

4. Storage and security

The app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. The Firebase project operator and Google may process information to provide those services. The project's region and retention settings depend on its configuration. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections; no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and requests

Information remains in the configured Firebase project until deleted by the user or service operator. You can stop using the app at any time. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information, email datedawnsupport@gmail.com. Requests may require identity verification and are handled subject to applicable law.

6. Children

Table Talk is not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required where they live. Do not use the service if you are below that age.

7. Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page and the date above will be updated.
