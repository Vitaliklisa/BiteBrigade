# Table Talk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This draft describes information handled by Table Talk, a restaurant decision
app. Before public release, the service operator should verify this policy
against the production configuration and applicable law.

## Information handled

When you use Table Talk, the configured Firebase project may store:

- Account identifiers, email address, and display name from sign-in.
- Rooms, meal times, descriptions, restaurant options and details, and notes
  that you create.
- Votes, participant roles, crew memberships, and invitations.
- In-app notification records about invitations and room activity.

Do not submit information you do not want shared with the people in the room
or crew.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, create and share restaurant
decisions, collect votes, show in-app updates, and protect the service.

## Sharing

Room information is available to the creator and accepted participants.
Information in a room shared with a crew is also available to that crew's
members. Invitation email addresses are used to address invitations. Table Talk
does not make room content public by default.

## Storage and service providers

The app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. The Firebase project
operator and Google may process information to provide those services. The
project's region and retention settings depend on the Firebase project
configuration. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections; no system can
guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and your choices

Information remains in the configured Firebase project until deleted by the
user or service operator. You can stop using the app at any time. To ask about
access, correction, or deletion of information, contact
[bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com](mailto:bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com). Requests may
require identity verification and are handled subject to applicable law.

## Children

Table Talk is not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required
where they live. Do not use the service if you are below that age.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in the published policy. For privacy
questions or requests, email [bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com](mailto:bitebrigadesupport@gmail.com).
